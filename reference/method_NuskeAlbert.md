# Vegetation End Method "NuskeAlbert"

The method `NuskeAlbert` provides a very simple method which is inspired
by standard climatological procedures but employs a 7 day moving average
and a 5 °C threshold (cf. Walther and Linderholm 2006).

## Calculations

2 criteria: temperature & short day criterion

### Temperature criterion

7 day moving average of daily mean temperatures end if 5 consecutive
days under under 5° C start the search at 1st of July / vegetation start

### Short day criterion

last day of the vegetation period is 5th of October
